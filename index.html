About

Preserving History, One Print at a Time

I got into collecting at eighteen, when my grandfather handed down part of his military collection — medals, insignia, a few original wartime pieces he'd held onto for years. Holding those objects and knowing the history behind them got me hooked.

LG Trading grew out of that same instinct — finding pieces worth preserving and getting them to people who actually appreciate them. Today that means original WWI and WWII posters, vintage show and cinema posters, and signed fine art prints.

Everything I sell is personally sourced and researched. No reproductions, no inflated stories — just the real piece and what's known about it.

"If you collect, or you just have an eye for things with history behind them, I'd love to hear from you."
